# Module 4: The AEGISAXIOM Ecosystem & Digital Workforce

## What AEGISAXIOM actually is

In Modules 1-3 you learned the philosophy: build AI workflows tailored to a customer's actual operations, instead of buying generic SaaS that forces them to adapt. This module is where you learn the platform you're actually selling — AEGISAXIOM — and the digital workforce it contains.

AEGISAXIOM is the central interface and orchestrator for SimpliMeta's AI employees. It's not a bundle of separate tools you stitch together. It's one platform that contains a fleet of specialized agents, governs them through a unified policy layer, and surfaces their work through a single dashboard. When you sell AEGISAXIOM to a prospect, you're not selling them six AI products — you're selling them a digital workforce that already includes the management infrastructure to run it.

The power of this framing is that it short-circuits the question every executive asks: *"How do we manage all this AI?"* The answer is: AEGISAXIOM manages it. Permissions, routing, audit logs, performance metrics, escalation paths — they all live one layer up from the individual agents. This is what makes AEGISAXIOM sellable to enterprise CFOs and not just to tech-forward startups.

## The 6 AEGISAXIOM agents (memorize these)

You need to know these six cold. They will come up in every Call 1 you ever do, and getting them confused — or forgetting one — instantly costs credibility with technically literate prospects.

**Orion** is the SOW Builder. Orion takes the structured intelligence from a completed discovery process (specifically, the AI Employee Matrix that Vega produces after Call 3) and drafts the full Statement of Work — executive summary, scope, milestones, financial justification, assumptions and risks. Orion doesn't do discovery; it consumes discovery output and turns it into a polished deliverable. Recent dashboard data: ~94% accuracy, 4.2 minute average completion time per SOW.

**Pulsar** is the rules engine. Pulsar evaluates thousands of policy rules in milliseconds, making it ideal for any workflow where high-throughput decisions need to be made against a complex ruleset. Insurance claims scoring, transaction fraud detection, compliance check routing — these are all Pulsar jobs. If a prospect describes a scenario where "we need to evaluate every X against Y rules in real time," you're hearing a Pulsar use case.

**Zenith** is the analytics layer. Zenith pulls data from across the AEGISAXIOM workspace and produces real-time dashboards, reports, and trend analysis. Where Pulsar makes operational decisions, Zenith surfaces strategic patterns. Executives love Zenith because it gives them visibility without requiring them to learn a BI tool.

**Lyra** handles outbound communication. Lyra sends emails, Slack messages, follow-ups, and notifications based on workflow events. It's the agent that bridges autonomous workflows and human stakeholders — when something happens that a person needs to know about, Lyra is the messenger. Customer support follow-ups, internal status updates, and triggered marketing comms are all Lyra's domain.

**Vega** is the AI Recruiter Agent — the discovery and research specialist you'll be partnering with as a certified rep. Vega analyzes call transcripts, identifies what you missed asking, surfaces workflow opportunities, and builds the AI Employee Matrix that ultimately feeds Orion. Vega is the agent you'll work with most directly. You're being trained right now to be its human partner.

**Callisto** is the internal support agent. Callisto answers questions about how to use AEGISAXIOM, surfaces documentation when users get stuck, and escalates to humans when needed. She's a Conversational AI handling support inside the platform, and she's one of the 9 of 11 currently active AI employees in the AEGISAXIOM workspace.

Memorize these six. Orion. Pulsar. Zenith. Lyra. Vega. Callisto. SOW. Rules. Analytics. Communication. Research. Support.

## The 3 AI archetypes

Beneath the named agents, there are three fundamental archetypes that all AEGISAXIOM workflows fall into. Knowing these helps you classify what kind of solution a prospect needs.

**Automated Workflows** are deterministic, event-triggered pipelines. A webhook fires, a row is inserted, a schedule hits — and a chain of steps executes. These are often built with frameworks like LangChain. They run when triggered, complete the work, and stop. Most "back office" automation falls into this archetype.

**Conversational AIs** are dialog-based agents that hold multi-turn conversations and dynamically call tools as needed. Vega is a Conversational AI. So is Callisto. So is ARC (the agent teaching you right now). They run as long as a human is talking to them, maintain state across turns, and select tools based on what the conversation requires.

**Web Apps** are structured human-facing interfaces. They exist because some workflows require humans to make discrete decisions — approve an expense, fill a form, review a proposal — and free-form chat is too ambiguous for that kind of interaction. Web Apps bridge the gap between fully autonomous workflows and conversational interaction, giving the human exactly the right amount of control for the task.

When a prospect describes a need, you should be classifying it: is this an Automated Workflow, a Conversational AI, or a Web App? Often the answer is "more than one" — but having the categories in your head means you can describe the solution in a way technical buyers immediately understand.

## Dashboard literacy

Here's a skill that separates good reps from great ones: reading the AEGISAXIOM dashboard out loud to a skeptical prospect and turning the numbers into ROI.

When a prospect points at the dashboard and sees "Orion: 94% accuracy, 4.2 minutes average completion," the temptation is to say "those are great numbers." That's flat. The right move is to translate: *"94% accuracy means human review is the exception, not the rule, so the labor savings on SOW drafting are real. 4.2 minutes vs the 4-hour human baseline gives you a velocity gain you can plug straight into your ROI model — that's roughly 57x faster, and you'll see exactly how to convert that into a dollar number when we get to MMI scoring on Call 3."*

This is dashboard literacy in action. You're not reciting stats — you're connecting them to the prospect's business case in real time. Reps who can do this consistently are the ones prospects trust to scope real engagements.
