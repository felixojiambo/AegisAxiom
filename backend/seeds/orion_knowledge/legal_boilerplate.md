# Legal Boilerplate for SOW Generation

## Standard Terms Reference
All Statements of Work are governed by the Master Services Agreement (MSA) between SimpliMeta and the Client. The SOW should reference the MSA by date and include the following standard clauses:

### Confidentiality
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information exchanged during the engagement. Client data processed by AI Employees is subject to SimpliMeta's Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

### Intellectual Property
- SimpliMeta retains ownership of all AI Employee platform technology, configurations, and models
- Client retains ownership of all client data and business-specific rules configured within the platform
- Custom integrations developed for the client are licensed for client use but owned by SimpliMeta

### Change Control
Any changes to scope, timeline, or pricing must be documented in a Change Order signed by both parties. Change Orders become addenda to this SOW.

### Acceptance Criteria
Each milestone deliverable will be subject to a 5-business-day acceptance period. If Client does not provide written acceptance or rejection within this period, the deliverable is deemed accepted.

### Limitation of Liability
SimpliMeta's total liability under this SOW shall not exceed the total fees paid under this SOW. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
